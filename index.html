Welcome to Mother (MTHR)

About Mother

Dive into the original altcoin that's always got a stash of snacks and zero bedtime rules. Mother (MTHR) is here to wrap you in a warm, digital hug and remind you of the good ol' days.

Tokenomics

  • Total Supply: 1,000,000,000 MTHR
  • 5% of each transaction goes to the Snack Fund
  • 3% of each transaction is distributed to all holders
  • 2% of each transaction is burned forever

How to Buy

  1. Get a Wallet - Download a reputable Ethereum wallet like MetaMask or Trust Wallet.
  2. Acquire ETH - Purchase Ethereum (ETH) from a cryptocurrency exchange like Coinbase or Binance.
  3. Connect to a DEX - Connect your wallet to a decentralized exchange (DEX) like Uniswap or SushiSwap.
  4. Swap ETH for MTHR - Use the DEX's swap feature to exchange your ETH for MTHR.
